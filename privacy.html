Lumo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Lumo is a camera app that helps you frame and edit aesthetic photos. This page explains what data the app handles.

No account, no tracking

Lumo has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell data.

Camera and photos

Lumo needs camera access to take photos. Photos you take stay on your device. Lumo only saves a photo to your library when you choose to save it, which needs photo library add access.

Composition suggestions (AI)

When you use the framing suggestion, Lumo sends a small, low resolution preview image from the viewfinder, plus the current zoom level, to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to suggest a crop and a short tip. The image is used only to produce that answer. We do not store the image and we do not use it to train models. Google processes the request under its own API terms.

Device identifier and usage limits

To prevent abuse and control costs, each request includes a random identifier for the app on your device (Apple's identifier for vendors). We store it together with a daily request counter. It is not linked to your name or any other personal data.

Purchases

Lumo Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription.

Retention and your rights

Usage counters are kept only as long as needed for rate limiting. Because we do not hold personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing to delete, but you can contact us any time with questions or requests.

Children

Lumo is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
